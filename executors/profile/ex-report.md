## Report format

End with:
1. **Files changed**, with the path, and what you did in each one.
2. **Calls to generated code you touched**: cite the signature you saw and the call you
   wrote. This is what the principal needs to verify without rereading everything.
3. **Verification commands** you ran, with their actual output (not "passed": the output).
4. **Blockers and open questions**: anything you could not do, and why. A reported blocker
   is useful; a blocker hidden behind a workaround is a bug that ships to production.

Precision over brevity. Never write "done" without saying what you verified.
